# Knowledge Vault

A living collection of things I learn, explore, build, and understand over time.
This repository contains distilled notes from books, courses, papers, projects, experiments, and concepts that I encounter throughout my learning journey.
The notes are written primarily in my own words and are intended to serve as both a reference and a record of what I have learned.

## Areas

- [Artificial Intelligence]
- [Data Structures]
- [Python]
- [Web Development]

## How I Learn
My notes are intentionally concise.
While learning, I first work through the material and then reconstruct what I remember in my own words. The resulting notes focus on the concepts and mental models that I want to retain rather than attempting to reproduce the source material.
More detailed examples, references, and intermediate material may remain in my personal knowledge base.

## Structure
Each area has its own organization depending on the nature of the subject.
- **Books**: notes and takeaways from books
- **Courses**: notes from courses and learning programs
- **Papers**:  notes and understanding from research papers
- **Projects**: knowledge and documentation gathered while building projects
- **Concepts**: individual concepts and topics
- **Base**: higher-level maps and connections between related notes

## Philosophy
> Learn → Recall → Explain → Connect → Refine

This repository is a growing record of that process.
